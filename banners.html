Home Promotions

5 fixed banner slots, each with hard-coded default copy that renders immediately. CleverTap Product Experience (remote config) variables can override every field and reorder the slots — without a redeploy.

Banner Sequence

Display order is driven entirely by the banner_seq remote config variable — a comma-separated list of slot numbers (1-5). Change it on the dashboard to promote, demote, or drop a banner.
